      CYBERCUP 6.0 / HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

      ThreatMeter is a CyberCup 6.0 hackathon prototype designed to demonstrate how suspicious messages can be assessed for common phishing indicators.

      ThreatMeter sends submitted messages to its backend for analysis by a machine-learning phishing classifier. Results are predictions, not guarantees, and depend on the model and its training data.

      Awareness tool, not a guarantee. This demo is educational and cannot guarantee that a message is safe or malicious. Always verify unexpected requests through a trusted, independent channel.
